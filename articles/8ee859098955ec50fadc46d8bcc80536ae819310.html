Prime Video has released its list of new shows and movies coming to Canada in August 2026 (it’s hard to believe July is almost over).

As for August highlights? Live sports coverage features four NWSL Friday night fixtures. Documentaries arriving on the service include Novak Djokovic: The Wolf in Winter on August 20, which examines the career of the 24-time Grand Slam tennis champion. Film streaming debuts include the drama Is God Is on August 19, the time-travel boy band feature Back to the 90s on August 21, the book adaptation drama The Last Sunrise starring Eva Longoria and Maia Reficco on August 26, and the small-town Minnesota crime story Normal on August 28.
