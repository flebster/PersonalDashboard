We now know what the Pixel 11 Pro Fold will look like following leaks that were uploaded online

The leak comes from notable leaker Evan Blass, who shared what looks to be marketing renders of the upcoming Pixel 11 Pro Fold to his Substack.

Now, if the renders are accurate, they show that the new foldable will look fairly similar to last year’s model. It will still use a tall outer screen rather than switching to a shorter style, as with the recently debuted Galaxy Z Fold 8 and the long-rumoured foldable iPhone. As The Verge’s Jay Peters pointed out, the renders also show that the inner screen will retain the 8-inch size.

Interestingly, the back camera design appears to have been slightly redesigned, and looks to get the “Pixel Glow”  that Google has already shown off for the Pixel 11 series.

Fans of more earthy tones will also be overjoyed, as the device has been shown off in olive green. Alongside the leaks, Blass leaked snapshots of what appear to be upcoming Pixel Watch and Pixel Buds models.

This leak follows several other major leaks for the Pixel 11 series, including an accidental leak by Google that appeared to reveal what the Pixel 11 would look like, and a major leak by Amazon that revealed the Pixel 11’s pricing and colours.

The next Made by Google hardware event takes place on August 12 at 6 p.m. ET, which is where these devices will most likely be revealed. If you’re thinking of getting a Pixel 11 when it launches, just know it is expected to cost more.
