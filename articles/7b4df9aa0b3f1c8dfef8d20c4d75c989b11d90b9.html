To start, the Coolify Cyber uses graphene's thermal conductivity (a layer of carbon atoms arranged in a hexagonal lattice) and cooling particles to reduce skin temperature—and it instantly felt cold when I put it around my neck.

Once powered on, the four internal bladeless fans start to rotate as six top air vents and two lower air vents push cool air down your neck, face, and upper back. There are three different cooling levels, and the Cyber is effective on its highest setting, but the lowest likely won't be enough for scorching days.

Connecting the neck fan to the companion app gives you access to more controls, such as a timer and power-level options, plus the ability to switch individual vents on and off.

The Cyber also features a long-lasting battery and quickly recharges using the included USB-C cable. While it is the most expensive option on our list, its features and excellent cooling capability make it worth the money.
