Wondering what to watch on streaming?

Every week, MobileSyrup outlines some of the most notable new movies and TV shows. We typically focus on new content from Amazon’s Prime Video, Crave, Disney+ and Netflix, but other services like Apple TV+ and Paramount+ will be mentioned when relevant. Premium video-on-demand (PVOD) platforms are also fair game as movies continue to come to digital early.

Finally, we’ll highlight shows or movies that are made by Canadian companies, involve notable Canadian cast or crew, and/or are filmed in Canada.

Apple TV

The Dink [Apple Original]

Apple TV premiere date: Friday, July 24
Genre: Sport comedy
Runtime: 1 hour, 43 minutes

An aging tennis pro goes against his instincts and plays pickleball to save a local country club.

The Dink was directed by Josh Greenbaum (Strays) and stars Jake Johnson (New Girl), Ben Stiller (Tropic Thunder), Mary Steenburgen (Melvin and Howard) and Ed Harris (The Truman Show).

Stream The Dink on Apple TV.

An Apple TV membership costs $14.99/month and is also included in Apple One subscription bundles, which start at $22.95/month.

Crave

Stuart Fails to Save the Universe

Crave premiere date: Thursday, July 23 (first episode, new episodes on Thursdays)
Genre: Comedy
Runtime: 10 episodes

In this latest The Big Bang Theory spin-off, Stuart accidentally damages a device made by Sheldon and Leonard, forcing him to embark on a dimension-hopping quest to save the multiverse.

Stuart Fails to Save the Universe was created by The Big Bang Theory‘s Chuck Lorre and Bill Prady alongside Zak Penn (Alphas) and features returning The Big Bang Theory stars Kevin Sussman (Stuart), Lauren Lapkus (Denise), Brian Posehn (Bert) and John Ross Bowie (Barry).

Stream Stuart Fails to Save the Universe on Crave.

An ad-free Crave Premium membership costs $22/month. Alternatively, there are two other subscription options: Crave Basic with Ads ($9.99/month) and Crave Standard with Ads ($14.99/month). Starz is available as a separate $5.99 add-on. You can learn more about subscriptions and add-ons on Crave’s website.

MobileSyrup also has a round-up of everything that’s coming to Crave in July.

Disney+

King of the Hill (Season 15) [Disney+ Original]

Disney+ Canada premiere date: Monday, July 20 (all episodes)
Genre: Animated sitcom
Runtime: 10 episodes

Hank and Peggy settle into retirement while Bobby navigates his food truck and a complicated romance with Connie.

King of the Hill was created by Mike Judge (Beavis and Butt-Head) and Greg Daniels (The Office) and features the voices of Judge, Kathy Najimy (Hocus Pocus), Pamela Adlon (Better Things), and Stephen Root (Barry).

Stream King of the Hill on Disney+.

Pompeii: Out of Time with Tom Hiddleston [Disney+ Original]

Disney+ Canada premiere date: Thursday, July 23 (all episodes)
Genre: Documentary
Runtime: Three episodes

Tom Hiddleston (Loki) talks with experts to determine whether anyone could have survived the destruction of Pompeii.

Stream Pompeii: Out of Time with Tom Hiddleston on Disney+.

Disney+ costs $8.99/month for Standard with Ads, $15.99/month or $159.99/year for Standard (no ads) and $16.99/month or $169.99/year for Premium.

MobileSyrup also has a round-up of everything coming to the service in July.

Netflix

72 Hours [Netflix Original]

Netflix Canada premiere date: Friday, July 24
Genre: Comedy
Runtime: 1 hour, 45 minutes

A 40-year-old mistakenly ends up at a wild Miami bachelor party with 20-somethings.

72 Hours was directed by Tim Story (Ride Along) and stars Kevin Hart (Jumanji series), Marcello Hernández (Saturday Night Live), Mason Gooding (Scream series), Kam Patterson (Saturday Night Live) and Montreal’s Michael Mando (Better Call Saul).

Stream 72 Hours on Netflix.

Netflix Standard with Ads costs $7.99/month, Standard (no ads, HD-supported) costs $18.99/month, and Premium (no ads, 4K-supported) costs $23.99/month. More information is available on Netflix’s website.

MobileSyrup also has a round-up of everything coming to Netflix Canada in July.

Paramount+

Epic: Elvis Presley in Concert

Original theatrical release date: February 20, 2026
Paramount+ Canada premiere date: Friday, July 24
Genre: Concert film
Runtime: 1 hour, 36 minutes

From Elvis director Baz Luhrmann comes this concert film featuring never-before-seen footage of the King of Rock and Roll during his Las Vegas residency.

Stream Epic: Elvis Presley in Concert on Paramount+.

Star Trek: Strange New Worlds (Season 4)

Paramount+ Canada premiere date: Thursday, July 23 (first episode, new episodes on Thursdays)
Genre: Sci-fi
Runtime: 10 episodes

Captain Pike leads the Enterprise crew on another series of adventures.

This Star Trek: Discovery spin-off was created by Discovery alums Akiva Goldsman, Alex Kurtzman, and Jenny Lumet and stars Discovery‘s Anson Mount (Pike), Ethan Peck (Spock) and Rebecca Romijin (Number One) alongside Jess Bush (Australia’s Next Top Model), Christina Chong (24: Live Another Day) and Celia Rose Gooding (Jagged Little Pill).  It’s also worth noting that the series was filmed in Mississauga.

Stream Star Trek: Strange New Worlds on Paramount+.

Paramount+ Basic (with ads) costs $6.99/month or $61.99/year, Standard costs $10.99/month or $97.99/year, and Premium costs $13.99/month or $124.99/year. MobileSyrup also has a round-up of what came to Paramount+ Canada in July.

Prime Video

Masters of the Universe

Original theatrical release date: June 5, 2026
Prime Video Canada premiere date: Wednesday, July 22
Genre: Sci-fi/fantasy
Runtime: 2 hours, 20 minutes

Prince Adam returns to Eternia to reclaim his destiny as He-Man and stop the evil Skeletor.

Based on the Mattel media franchise of the same name, Masters of the Universe was directed by Travis Knight (Bumblebee) and stars Nicholas Galitzine (Bottoms), Camila Mendes (Riverdale), Idris Elba (Luther), Jared Leto (Morbius) and Alison Brie (Community).

Stream Masters of the Universe on Prime Video.

Prime Video is included with an Amazon Prime membership, which costs $99/year. An additional $2.99/month fee is required to remove ads.

MobileSyrup also has a round-up of everything coming to the service in July.

It’s also worth noting that MobileSyrup is running a contest in partnership with Prime Video to give away a special edition Masters of the Universe Amazon Fire TV Stick. You have until July 29 to enter.
