This is a net‑style panel covered with 200 small LEDs in alternating orange and purple, spanning about 8.2×4.9 feet, ideal for draping over bushes, trees, or fences. It offers eight lighting modes, a built‑in timer function, and memory, so it powers up using the last mode used. The lights are UL‑certified, and they’re connectable—so you can link multiple nets together to cover larger surfaces.

Rated waterproof for use in wet and cold climates, this net gives a bold splash of Halloween color with flexibility, mode variety, and relatively easy installation.
