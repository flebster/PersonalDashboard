There are a lot of kid-friendly headphones on the market to choose from. Compared to adult headphones, kids’ headphones are designed to fit young heads more comfortably and generally include volume-limiting features to protect their ears. PCMag’s top pick is the iClever BTH20 ANC headphones for kids, currently 30% off at a record-low price of $34.98 (originally $49.99).

These headphones earned a PCMag Editor’s Choice Award thanks to their comfortable fit and ability to blend passive and active noise cancellation, allowing for lower-volume listening. The maximum volume limit is 80dB, which protects your kids' ears, and their child-friendly design and fit, exceptional battery life, and lower volume limit make them a top contender. Their frequency response curve deliberately dips in the 2kHz to 4kHz range to limit exposure to frequencies that can be harmful at high volumes.

The headphones have 40mm drivers that offer a frequency response of 20Hz to 20kHz. ANC, which reduces ambient noise by up to 40dB when combined with passive isolation, is the main high-end feature here. Notably, there are two noise-canceling settings available: high and low. The low setting blocks out deep and droning sounds like a fan, while the high setting reduces repetitive low-end noise. They connect via Bluetooth 5.4 and support AAC, making them a good choice for Apple devices. 

The iClever BTH20 headphones feature pivoting, fold-flat leatherette earcups that are plush and easy to store, and come with a protective case for traveling. They're lightweight at 7.69 ounces, and are available in five kid-friendly colors. Kids should find the tactile buttons with debossed icons easy to identify, but it’s worth noting they don’t have an IPX rating, so they should be kept away from water. 

What do you think so far?

Battery life lasts up to 60 hours with ANC off and up to 35 hours with ANC on, meaning that even if your child forgets to turn the headphones off after use, they’ll still last a while. Plus, a 10-minute top-up gives you up to five hours of listening time. In short, if you want ultra-comfy kids’ headphones on the market that help protect young ears while still offering noise cancellation, the iClever BTH20 ANC headphones for kids are your best bet.
