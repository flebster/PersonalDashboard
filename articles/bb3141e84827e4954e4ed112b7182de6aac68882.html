The Milwaukee M18 Fuel is one of the longest-lasting string trimmers I’ve evaluated and has the cutting power necessary to clear knee-high, dense weeds and take down saplings. With two large 8Ah batteries strapped to its back, you can get upwards of 30 minutes out of this beast running on low, or about 20 minutes with the speed cranked up.

That may not sound like a lot, but it’s pretty good considering it swings a beefy 0.105-inch trimmer line, cutting a 17-inch area with enough force to bring down small saplings. Just keep in mind that two big batteries make for a heavy trimmer: It’s more than 16 pounds, so be prepared for an upper body workout.

Along with its power and battery life, the M18 Fuel brings the quality-of-life features you’d expect from a Milwaukee tool. Its easy-load trimmer head can refill 25 feet of trimmer line in less than 30 seconds. A variable speed trigger lets you slow down to work around plantings and structures.

Milwaukee tools aren't cheap, and this string trimmer is no exception. Much of the cost comes from those high-capacity batteries, though: If you’re already bought into the Milwaukee system and own a few 18-volt batteries, the bare version of this tool is substantially cheaper.
