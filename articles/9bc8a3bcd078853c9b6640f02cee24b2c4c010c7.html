After President Joe Biden outraged millions of Christians by declaring Easter Sunday as the “Transgender Day of Visibility,” former Republican representative David Jolly stood by him. 

Jolly, now running for governor in Florida as a Democrat, did a media tour to defend Biden and lecture conservatives about their theology. Now, Democrats are running him to replace Ron DeSantis as governor of Florida as he attempts to sweep culture war issues under the rug. 

Many Democrats have been quiet on the transgender issues this election cycle after polling showed that the issue hampered Kamala Harris in 2024. Jolly was vocal about the topic during his post-Republican days, attacking his former party for passing laws banning transgender procedures on kids and keeping gender-confused boys off of girls’ sports teams.

Jolly even vigorously defended Biden after he called in March 2024 for Americans on Easter Sunday to “join us in lifting up the lives and voices of transgender people throughout our Nation.” Biden’s decision prompted significant pushback from prominent Republicans like House Speaker Mike Johnson and the Trump campaign. Jolly said their politics and theology were wrong. 

“The Jesus they celebrate today would be inclusive of the transgender community, and would be supportive of the transgender community,” he said while appearing on MSNBC, adding that “all Christians, including Donald Trump, Mike Johnson, and others who celebrate today, that, in fact, they should love the transgender community as they love their own God. I am not sure the theology upon which they rely to criticize Joe Biden.”

“I think it’s wrong theologically, it’s wrong morally, and it’s absolutely stupid politically,” he added. 

In another appearance on the Stephanie Miller Happy Hour podcast, he said conservatives turned Biden’s proclamation into the latest “culture war” flash point. 

“First, theologically and doctrinally, I think the Right is wrong when it comes to biblical theology. I think they’re a long ways from what Jesus would do, the bracelets they used to wear,” he said. “ The reality is Jesus is a loving God who would support the trans community and be proud of their bravery and the struggle that they have gone through for recognition and equity.”

In other media appearances reviewed by The Daily Wire, Jolly attacked a law shielding kids from transgender procedures and claimed that kids would be ripped away from their parents if they obtained transgender medications from outside of Florida. 

“That’s the real consequence of this war on gender-affirming care and the trans community,” he said on the Joe Walsh podcast. “I don’t have a personal appreciation or understanding of what all that is like. But I know what it would feel like if the state stepped in and say, ‘Hey, Jolly, you’re not allowed to make decisions for your kid?’”

Jolly also said he was fine with biological boys competing on girls’ sports teams during an interview with radio host Charlie Sykes. 

“I think it’s been pretty disgusting what we’ve seen from Republicans in the past several years on this. Look, I don’t think we need to knock down that youth soccer player who is working with their family and their doctor on whatever their current, you know, crisis is personally,” he said. “I think it can be supportive of them to allow them to play in youth sports.”

Ultimately he said he thought that the case should be decided by the Supreme Court.

“I think some latitude among the sports leagues to make decisions, but ultimately, it’s a constitutional question that the courts are going to have to decide,” he said. 

Jolly, whose issues page on his website does not mention “transgender” or LGBTQ, recently predicted that he would be criticized by Republicans for being pro-transgender.

The Democrat is expected to face off against Rep. Byron Donalds, who was endorsed by President Donald Trump. Donalds has a large lead against GOP rivals including James Fishback, Lt. Governor Jay Collins, and former Florida House Speaker Paul Renner.
