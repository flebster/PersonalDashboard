Paramount+ has shared its list of new shows and movies coming to Canada in August 2026, which also includes new titles coming to its ad-supported streaming service Pluto TV.

Highlights for the month include the Season 3 premiere of Lioness on August 2, the debut of psychological drama Mother Mary on August 4, and the launch of The Challenge Season 42 on August 5. Viewers can also follow weekly episodes of Star Trek: Strange New Worlds, The Westies, and Tyler Perry’s Ruthless alongside live sports with Zuffa Boxing #10 on August 8. The month rounds out with full-season drops for Serial Killer Wives and Average Joe, the movie release of Fuze, and the series premiere of Dalliance on August 27. We’re looking forward to the new season of Lioness next month.
