As you’ll see throughout this list, we believe in SentrySafe as a top brand for fireproof safes—for its lifetime warranty, $50,000 fire protection guarantee, and reliably high levels of fire protection across all products. This mid-sized, 1.23-cubic-foot option is the perfect choice for most people.

Alongside a fire rating of up to 1,700 degrees F for a full hour, the safe boasts a fully waterproof design that protects internal items completely for up to 24 hours in up to 8 inches of submerged water.

Beyond the powerful protection it offers, the fireproof safe also has internal organization features like key hooks, a shelf, and a lockable drawer for especially valuable items. The interior of the safe is even lit to help you see inside. That said, both the lights and the digital keypad are battery-operated, so you'll need to periodically replace them.

The safe, as a whole, weighs close to 90 pounds. While this is a great deterrent from a thief trying to simply steal the entire safe, it can make it quite difficult install or carry up stairs on your own.
