Raising chickens takes a lot of work, so it’s never lost on me what an incredible, nutrient-dense source of protein each little egg provides! Now, I know most of us don’t keep chickens at home (and I can tell you—it’s almost certainly more budget-friendly not to. Ha!). Pair that budget-friendly win with this recipe for cottage cheese eggs, and you truly can’t lose!

The addition of protein-packed cottage cheese is all it takes to make your classic scrambled eggs extra creamy and delicious. It’s protein added to protein! Which also means it’ll keep you FULL and doesn’t cost a ton, either! I add just a couple spoonfuls, which is enough to make the eggs soft and tender without adding too much moisture to the pan. I also like to gently mix in the cottage cheese so a few soft curds remain for a super creamy bite. Even if you’re not planning to hit the gym, this easy breakfast is going to make you very happy!

  1. Whisk the eggs really well! Beat the eggs and salt until the mixture is an even sunny yellow and looks slightly airy. This helps the eggs cook into softer, more uniform curds instead of leaving behind firm streaks of egg white.
  2. Adjust the heat to your stovetop. During testing, I found that my pan needed to be on the medium heat setting at our test kitchen, but medium-low was sufficient on my stovetop at home. If you feel like your cottage cheese scrambled eggs are taking too long to cook, and the texture is getting a little broken, kick the heat up a little and stop moving them around in the pan for a minute. Flip or stir once you see the eggs are cooking into more solid curds.
  3. Stir gently, then let the eggs cook. Use slow, sweeping motions to move the cooked egg aside and bring the liquid egg into contact with the pan. Try not to stir constantly, since the eggs need a few short pauses against the hot surface to form proper curds and allow excess moisture to cook away.
  4. Don’t mistake the cottage cheese for uncooked egg white! Because I add it after the eggs are fully whisked, a few small curds will be visible in the finished eggs. That texture is completely intentional and adds a creamy mouthfeel. If you prefer a totally smooth result, you can blend the egg mixture before cooking, but it’ll likely have a different texture than the version I’m sharing here.

Cottage Cheese Eggs

These easy Cottage Cheese Eggs are soft, protein-packed, and made with just 4 ingredients. A quick 15-minute breakfast that’s perfect for busy mornings!

  • Gather your ingredients.

  • Whisk the eggs well, and then whisk them some more! With a fork or a whisk, beat the eggs and salt together until the entire mixture is a consistent sunny yellow color, with no defined streaks of egg whites visible.

  • Add the cottage cheese.

  • Gently whisk the cottage cheese into the eggs, being careful to leave some of their natural texture intact. I think it adds to a pleasant mouthfeel to leave some of the curds!

  • Add butter to a skillet over medium-low heat. Once melted and a little foamy, add the cottage cheese egg mixture.

  • Be patient, let the eggs come up to temperature and begin to stir them in the pan once you see the color of your eggs turn from yellow to a more creamy bright yellow. Gently stir to move around the uncooked liquidy part of the egg to expose it to the pan. Don’t constantly stir, or you’ll end up with a sloppy mess. This doesn’t take long though, so don’t walk away!

  • Once the eggs are cooked, set but not tough or browning, remove them from the heat and enjoy!**

how to make Cottage Cheese Eggs Step-by-Step Photos

Gather all of your ingredients.

Whisk the eggs: Crack 3 large eggs into a medium bowl and add ⅛ tsp salt. Whisk thoroughly until the mixture is an even, sunny yellow with no visible streaks of egg white. Whisking well helps create softer, more evenly cooked eggs.

Add the cottage cheese: Add 2 Tbsp cottage cheese to the eggs (I use 4% cottage cheese).

Gently whisk just until it is distributed throughout the mixture. Leave some of the cottage cheese curds intact to give the finished eggs a soft and creamy texture.

Cook the eggs: Add 1 Tbsp butter to a nonstick skillet and place it over medium-low heat. Let the butter melt and become lightly foamy, but don’t let it brown. Pour the cottage cheese and egg mixture into the skillet. Let it sit undisturbed for several seconds, or until the eggs around the edges and across the bottom begin to turn from glossy yellow to a creamy, opaque yellow.

Gently scramble the eggs: Use a wooden spatula (or chopsticks!) to slowly push the cooked egg from the edges toward the center of the skillet. Tilt the pan slightly as needed so the uncooked egg can flow into the empty spaces and make contact with the hot surface. Stir only occasionally rather than continuously, which helps the eggs form soft, tender curds instead of becoming loose or mushy.

Continue gently folding and pushing the eggs around the skillet until they’re mostly set but still look slightly glossy and moist. This should only take a few minutes, so stay close to the stove!

Serve: Take the skillet off the heat before the eggs look completely dry, as they’ll continue cooking slightly from the residual heat. Serve your scrambled eggs with cottage cheese immediately, while they’re hot and delicious! Enjoy.

Serving Suggestions

I like to finish these cottage cheese scrambled eggs with a sprinkle of fresh chopped chives for a little extra flavor and color, but that’s totally optional. Sliced green onions, a crack of black pepper, or a few shakes of hot sauce are all great choices, too.

For a fuller breakfast, serve them with air fryer bacon, which cooks in about the same amount of time, along with fresh fruit or buttered toast (I love mine with tomato butter, personally!). A couple of reheated sheet pan pancakes are another fun option if you keep a batch tucked away in the freezer. Then add a glass of cold brew coffee, and breakfast is officially handled.

Storage & Reheating

These cottage cheese eggs are best when they’re freshly made. If you do have leftovers, let them cool, then transfer them to an airtight container and refrigerate for 3-4 days. I recommend making them fresh whenever possible, since scrambled eggs can become firmer once chilled and reheated.

Reheating

Reheat the eggs in the microwave in 20-30 second intervals, stirring between each one, until heated through. Keep a close eye on them, as reheating for too long can make the eggs overcooked and rubbery. Any leftover cottage cheese eggs would be delicious stirred into veggie fried rice for an easy dinner later in the day!
