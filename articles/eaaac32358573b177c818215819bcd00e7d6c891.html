This car seat cover is designed to provide support and keep you cool and comfortable by absorbing sweat and dissipating heat. It's filled with odorless, eco-friendly buckwheat hulls, with memory foam for cushioning and a breathable, linen-cotton blend fabric used for the cover.

Silica gel beads on the back keep it in place, and there are separate pieces for the headrest, seat, and back. The cover is said to work with 96 percent of cars on the road and comes in gray, black, and beige; it also gets an average 4.6 stars from nearly 1,000 customers.
