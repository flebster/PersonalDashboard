We were pleasantly surprised by the power and value offered by this small portable air conditioner from Black+Decker. It begins producing cold air very quickly, though not quite at the volume of many other air conditioners we tested. That said, it still reduced the temperature of its target at a rapid rate.

The unit provides multiple fan speeds, dedicated dehumidification, a timer setting, and a sleep setting. Note that there is no heater setting on this appliance. It’s meant to cool and dehumidify exclusively.

Reviews Editor Amber Joglar tested out the four-in-one version of this model which also functions as a heater. After just 10 minutes of use in her room, Joglar felt, “a noticeable shift in the air temperature.”

The quick cooldown times stood out across both models, though Joglar also pointed out that this led to the exhaust pipes heating up quickly as well. This isn’t necessarily a problem, but if you plan on using the unit for several hours a day, consider covering the tubes in insulation.

In addition to the cold air, Joglar liked the remote and LED control panel, saying, “I normally just change settings on the air conditioner itself because it’s right next to my bed, so backlit controls are helpful.”

Similarly, the remote control is great for when she’s already in bed or wants to pre-cool the room before she’s ready to go to sleep.

Joglar’s model also heats rooms. So, instead of storing it in a closet or attic for the cold winter months, she simply switches functionality.
