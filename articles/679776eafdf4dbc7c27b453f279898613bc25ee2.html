Whether you’ve just begun venturing into the world of robot vacuums or are looking to upgrade to a new model, the mid-range Eufy Omni C20 checks a lot of boxes. It's a vacuum/mop combo with some of the same high-end features offered by its more expensive competitors, and though it has smaller tanks and simpler sensors, that gives it a more compact silhouette—and a more affordable price tag. And right now, the self-sufficient Eufy Omni C20 robot vacuum and mop is down to an all-time low price of $339 (originally $549) on Amazon.

At around 13 by 13 by 3.5 inches, the Omni C20 can clear low-profile furniture. It has a number of useful features, including a dock that self-empties the dustbin, washes and dries the mop pads, and refills the robot with water. Unlike pricier models, it does not have advanced obstacle avoidance or more sophisticated mop management, so mopping is better for light once-overs and routine maintenance than removing dried-on spills and stains. The lack of LiDAR is also part of the trade-off for the lower price tag.

The polished Eufy app makes the Omni C20 easy to set up, control, and map. While its 7,000Pa of suction isn’t as high as some more premium competitors, it can still hold its own when cleaning (especially with its detangling roller brush), making it suitable for homes with pets with shorter hair, but perhaps not ideal for those with heavy shedders. The 2.4-liter dirty water tank lasts around a week without requiring emptying, and battery life varies based on how much square footage it’s covering, with a maximum of 950 square feet on its lowest suction setting.
