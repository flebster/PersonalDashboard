  • 8 ounces sliced mushrooms
  • 1 small bunch celery
  • 1 medium bag baby carrots
  • 1 small bag shredded carrots (or 2 small whole)

  • 1 (6-ounce) package sliced Nova lox (smoked salmon)

  • Chili powder

  • Hickory liquid smoke
  • BBQ sauce

  • Crushed red pepper flakes

  • 1 dozen large eggs
  • 1 pint liquid egg whites
  • 1 (9-inch) deep dish pie crust
  • 1 (16-ounce) container low fat or whole milk cottage cheese (I like Good Culture)

  • 1 small box unsalted butter
  • 1 small package blue cheese
