The Arc is Leatherman's premium offering, featuring a MagnaCut main blade and a 420 HC steel frame. There are 20 different tools that can be deployed with one hand thanks to the brand's proprietary "Free" technology.

Each tool locks into place for safety and is backed by the brand's lifetime guarantee. It's relatively lightweight, weighing just over half a pound, and has a closed length of just over four inches. For all its utility, it's surprisingly nimble. I have the carrying case for it, but it's plenty comfortable to carry by itself, clipped onto my jean pocket.

It was also dubbed “best overall” by Reviews Editor Tom Price when he tested a slew of multitools from brands like Gerber, Victorinox, and RoverTac.

“It’s hard to nail down one aspect of the Arc that I like best, because it does just about everything well,” notes Price. “Effectiveness across the board is precisely why it is our new best overall pick.”
