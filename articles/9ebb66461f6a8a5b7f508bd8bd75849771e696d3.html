Apple Canada has introduced a few new offers for the latest back to school season.

First off, you can get gift cards of various values when you buy the following products with Apple’s education discount:

  • MacBook Pro — $210
  • MacBook Air — $140
  • iPad Pro — $140
  • iPad Air — $140

For context, here are the prices of these products with Apple’s education savings:

  • MacBook Pro — starting at $2,659
  • MacBook Air — starting at $1,659
  • iPad Pro — starting at $1,559
  • iPad Air — starting at $929

Notably, Apple changed how its education discount works in May 2026, so customers will now need to verify their education status using Unidays in order to get the discount.
