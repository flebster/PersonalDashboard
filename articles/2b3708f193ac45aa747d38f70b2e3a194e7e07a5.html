Got the cash to splurge and demand the fastest Wi-Fi available? Then go for the Surfboard G54, which is a beast in just about every respect.

This DOCSIS 3.1 modem-router combo is compatible with Wi-Fi 7, the latest standard just launched in late 2023. It’s approved for internet plans up to 10 Gbps but can deliver Wi-Fi speeds of up to a blistering 18 Gbps. If that wasn’t enough, there are five Ethernet ports—one of which delivers 10 Gbps (the other four provide 1 Gbps).

There is one thing that doesn’t quite match up to the G54’s impressive credentials: its Wi-Fi range, which offers only a paltry 5,000 square feet.

That said, we’re excited about the G54, which is among the first of a new wave of Wi-Fi 7 devices. Of course, make sure your plan includes lightning-fast internet before you invest in this superb box of tricks.
