Gameloft has partnered with the Nature Conservancy of Canada (NCC) to raise money for the conservation charity through its popular Disney Dreamlight Valley life sim game.

From July 29 to September 23, Disney Dreamlight Valley will feature a special C$5.39 bundle that includes a special Grizzly Bear Cub Companion and 800 Moonstones. Gameloft has pledged to donate all proceeds of this bundle to NCC.

As Canada’s largest national land conservation organization, NCC works with partners across the country to preserve plants and wildlife. This mission is particularly important in recent years amid global warming, the effects of which we can see in massive wildfires across Canada. More information on the NCC and its work can be found on the non-profit’s website.

Disney Dreamlight Valley is available on PS5, PS54, Xbox Series X/S, Xbox One, Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch, PC, Mac and iOS (Apple Arcade). It’s worth noting that the NCC charity bundle coincides with the game’s July 29 Pixel Perfect update, which adds Wreck-It Ralph to the game. Gameloft will also hold a panel at Disney’s D23 fan event on August 14 to reveal more content that’s coming to the game later this year.
