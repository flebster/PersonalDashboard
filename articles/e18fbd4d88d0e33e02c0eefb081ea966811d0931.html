Juicy grilled Shrimp and Pineapple Skewers with a sweet-spicy soy glaze make a quick and flavorful summer meal for a poolside party or weeknight dinner.

Shrimp and Pineapple Kabobs

These Shrimp and Pineapple Kabobs are one of my favorite summer grilling recipes. Juicy shrimp and sweet pineapple chunks are brushed with a simple glaze of soy sauce, garlic, and a little sambal oelek for just the right amount of heat. The combination of sweet, savory, and spicy flavors is always a hit, and since shrimp cooks in just a few minutes, this easy dinner comes together fast.

Why This Shrimp and Pineapple Skewers Works

They’re perfect for busy weeknights, summer entertaining, or anytime you want something light, fresh, and packed with flavor without spending a lot of time in the kitchen.

  • Quick: You don’t have to marinate the shrimp, and they cook in under 8 minutes.
  • Sweet and spicy: The pineapple caramelizes as it cooks, creating a perfect contrast with the spicy sauce.
  • Excellent for summer: A grilling recipe that uses fresh summer produce
  • Great for backyard parties: Easy to throw a bunch on the grill

 

Ingredients You’ll Need

Besides the ingredients listed below, you’ll also need 8 small or 4 long skewers. If they’re wooden, soak them in water for at least 30 minutes to prevent them from burning on the grill. See the recipe card below for the exact measurements.

  • Shrimp and pineapple sauce: Sambal oelek (a spicy Southeast Asian chili sauce), toasted sesame oil, low-sodium soy sauce.
  • Extra large shrimp: Peel and devein the shrimp. Or, better yet, buy them ready to go from the supermarket. If you use frozen shrimp, don’t forget to thaw them in the fridge the day before.
  • Pineapple: Fresh pineapple is best for this recipe. Canned pineapple will turn mushier when grilled and won’t caramelize as well.
  • Red onion becomes sweeter when grilled.

 

How to Make Shrimp and Pineapple Kabobs

If you want to prepare some of it ahead of time, you can make the sauce, cut the pineapple and onions, and soak the skewers the day before. See the recipe card at the bottom for printable directions.

  1. Coat the shrimp, pineapple, and red onion in the sambal, sesame oil, and soy sauce. Then, thread each piece onto the skewers.
  2. Prep the grill: Preheat the outdoor grill to medium-high heat and oil the grates. An easy way to do this is to pour some avocado oil on a wad of paper towels and rub them across the grates. 
  3. Grill the kabobs: Spray them with oil and cook for 2 minutes on each side.

How do I cook shrimp skewers if I don’t have a grill?

If you don’t have a grill, cook these shrimp and pineapple kabobs on a grill pan or cast-iron skillet on the stove over medium-high heat until the shrimp are opaque. You can even skip the skewers if you want.

You can also cook everything in the oven at 350°F for 10 to 15 minutes or air fry them at 400°F for 5 to 6 minutes.

Variations

  • Spicy sauce: Swap sambal with sriracha.
  • Keep it gluten-free by using tamari instead of soy sauce.
  • Allergic to shrimp? Substitute chicken thighs, salmon, or flank steak. I’ve linked some other skewer recipes so you can see the specific cooking times for each type of protein. 
  • Add an extra vegetable, like zucchini, squash, or bell peppers.

Storage

  • Refrigerate the shrimp kabobs for up to 3 days.
  • Reheat: Remove the shrimp and pineapple from the skewer, then reheat in the microwave or in a skillet over medium-low heat.
