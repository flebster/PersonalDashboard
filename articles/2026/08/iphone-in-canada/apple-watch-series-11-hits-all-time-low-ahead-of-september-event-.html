Ahead of Apple’s September event, where a refresh for the Apple Watch lineup is expected, the current Series 11 GPS has dropped to what we’re seeing as an all-time low.

So where is the big sale? It’s available online from Staples Canada. Check out the prices below:

Apple Watch Series 11

  • 46mm GPS: $399 (save $150)
  • 42mm GPS: $439 (save $150)

At these prices, they’re cheaper than what we saw during Amazon’s Prime Day.

The Apple Watch Series 11 offers 24-hour battery life (it’s still not great), a more scratch-resistant display, and extended health features including sleep scores and hypertension alerts.

The upcoming Series 12 is rumoured to bring an upgraded S11 chip for faster performance and on-device AI, a new magnetic band attachment system, and the possible return of a scratch-resistant ceramic case option.
