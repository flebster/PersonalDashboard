You can get a 2023 Cadillac Lyriq for just over $35,000

A mid-size luxury SUV for the price of a RAV4

For the most part, electric vehicles depreciate pretty harshly in their first couple of years on the road. This might be less than ideal for those that are aiming to buy one new, but it means that those willing to buy used have access to some fantastic deals. One such deal is the 2023 Cadillac Lyriq. Despite it being only three years old, there are well-cared for examples that go for the same price as a new mid-trim RAV4.

While new EV prices are slowly but surely coming down, large models like the Lyriq still demand a pretty high price. Back when it was new, the 2023 model year started at $59,990, which was actually a lot cheaper than other mid-size luxury EVs. Despite undercutting its rivals, we think that it was among the best options you could buy, coming loaded with tech and high-end luxuries. Three years later, you can get the 2023 Lyriq for half price on the used market.

When it launched, Cadillac only offered the Lyriq in one fully-loaded trim. This means that the only choice you have to make when shopping for a used model is whether you want the single-motor, rear-wheel drive model or the dual-motor, all-wheel drive model. While dual-motor models do list for a slightly higher price, we think they are well worth the money, delivering some impressive amounts of power and stout towing capabilities.

Cadillac delivers a sleek, tech-focused cabin with the Lyriq

High-end luxury on par with its competitors

Despite being cheaper than its rivals, the Cadillac Lyriq is every bit as luxurious on the inside. The American automaker has clearly found their stride with their new electric lineup, and the Lyriq showcases some of the best and most impressive features. It looks great, inside and out, and also offers tons of interior room for passengers and cargo.

In terms of passenger space, the Lyriq is just as good as any of its rivals. While its spec sheet shows that headroom isn't particularly generous, even especially tall passengers will find that they fit quite comfortably. The trunk here might not be quite as large as what you'd get from some other mid-size luxury EVs, but there is still more than enough room for a couple of suitcases.

Cadillac has really nailed the interior design of the Lyriq. It comes richly appointed with plush materials, such as bright metallic trim and open-pore wood. Even the storage tray under the floating center console is lined with leather upholstery. You get a lot of standard luxuries here as well, including things like power-adjustable massaging front seats, heated and ventilated front seats, heated rear seats, a panoramic fixed glass roof, and ambient interior lighting.

Infotainment and technology

The focal point of the Lyriq's dashboard is its massive 33-inch curved advanced LED display. This single screen serves as both the digital gauge cluster and the infotainment system. Graphics are incredibly crisp, and the system is very easy to navigate. The infotainment system can be controlled via touch inputs or a rotary dial on the center console.

All the tech features you would expect in a high-end luxury EV are present here, including wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, a wireless smartphone charging pad, and Wi-Fi hotspot capability. The infotainment system includes Google built-in, meaning you can natively access things like Google Maps and Google Assistant. You also get a fantastic 19-speaker AKG sound system, which includes active noise cancellation.

As we touched on, the only choice you have to make when buying your used Lyriq is whether you want the single-motor or dual-motor setup. While you get a little more range by opting for the 340-horsepower, rear-wheel drive model, we think that the extra power and versatility you get from the all-wheel drive model is well worth the upgrade. Cadillac has leaned fully into the luxury identity of the Lyriq, meaning that it doesn't convey much sportiness. We don't really think this is a problem, though, as it is meant to be an SUV that you cruise around quietly in. This is further emphasized by its regenerative braking, which allows for one-pedal driving in most scenarios.

Every 2023 Lyriq comes equipped with the same 102-kWh battery pack, which means that every model is capable of over 300 miles on a single charge. Rear drive models do go a little further before their battery is depleted, but we're impressed with how well all-wheel drive models do, considering they put down a lot more power.

You don't have to spend big to get a luxurious electric SUV

If you're not scared of a couple of miles on the odometer, used luxury EVs offer some of the best value for money that you can get. The Cadillac Lyriq demonstrates this perfectly. It comes with a comfortable and spacious interior that is packed to the brim with top-notch materials and innovative technology, but it lists for the same price as a new Toyota RAV4. Capitalizing on the heavy early depreciation of EVs means you can get a lot of car for your money.
