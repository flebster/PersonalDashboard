Back in 1966, a store named Sound of Music first opened its doors. All these years later, that same store—long since renamed "Best Buy"— is celebrating its 60th anniversary with a big sale. And some of the deals can only be found in person.

How long will Best Buy's "60th Anniversary Sale" last?

Best Buy's 60th Anniversary Sale will run from Monday, Aug. 17 through Sunday, Aug. 23. The sale will be both in-store and online, with fresh deals on the weekend from Aug. 21-23.

Do you need to be a member to shop Best Buy's sale?

You don't need to be any kind of member to shop Best Buy's 60th Anniversary Sale; however, it is free to sign up for a My Best Buy membership, which will get you free shipping. If you join one of their paid subscriptions (My Best Buy Plus for $49.99 per year or My Best Buy Total for $179.99 per year), you'll get faster free shipping, exclusive prices and deals, an extended 60-day return window on most products, and early access to select sales. Paid members will also earn extra rewards from both the current sale and the upcoming sales.

What deals can I expect during Best Buy's 60th Anniversary Sale?

You can find deals on TVs, laptops, headphones, video games, electric bikes, and other tech products like back-to-school deals, headphones, and laptops. On Saturday, Aug. 22, Best Buy will have six in-person exclusive deals, including a deal on gift cards, that will be limited while supplies last.

You can get Beats' Powerbeats Pro 2 for $169.95 (originally $249.99), the lowest they've been. These are great workout earbuds, according to Lifehacker senior health editor Beth Skwarecki's review.
