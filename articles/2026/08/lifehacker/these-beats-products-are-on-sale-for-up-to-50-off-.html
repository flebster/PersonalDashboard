Beats Studio Pro Premium wireless over-ear headphones are over 50% off

The Beats Studio Pro Premium wireless over-ear headphones are now 51% off, making them $169.95, down from $349.99. These Beats also come with their own carrying case.

Beats Solo 4 wireless on-ear Bluetooth headphones are less than $100

The Beats Solo 4 wireless on-ear Bluetooth headphones in metallic pink, compatible with both iOS and Android devices, are now $99.95, down from $149.99. They also have ear cushions that are designed to be comfortable for long listening sessions. When fully charged, the headphones have a battery life of up to 50 hours. For people who prefer earbuds to headphones, the Beats Powerbeats Pro 2 wireless earbuds are now $199.95  (originally $249.99).
