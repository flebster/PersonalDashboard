The previous Amazon Fire HD 8 tablet came out in 2022, with this latest version adding two extra GBs of RAM for a total of 4GB. The Fire HD 8 gets its name from the 8-inch screen display with 1,280 by 800 pixels; it also has a Hexa-core 2.0 GHz processor, a resolution of 1,280 by 800 pixels, about 13 hours of battery life, a brighter screen than the older version, and a 64GB storage capacity with the possibility to expand it to 1TB if you want to buy more separately. The front camera is the same 2MP with 720p HD video recording, but the rear camera is now a 5MP lens with 1080p HD video recording.

What do you think so far?

The high-definition touch screen is not amazing by any means, but for an $89.99 tablet, you'll get great value for your money if you're just looking for something you can carry around to watch your shows or browse online. This is a great budget tablet, but keep in mind that since it is a Fire Tablet, you'll be limited in terms of apps within Amazon's own app store (unless you download the Google Play Store).
