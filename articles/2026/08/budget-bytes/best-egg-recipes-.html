If there is one thing that I always keep around, it’s eggs. Not only are they inexpensive and easy to cook, but there are so many ways to enjoy them. Scrambled, fried, poached, hard or soft-boiled, on their own, packed with veggies, loaded with melty cheese, or served with meat…the possibilities for eggs are really endless. That’s why they’re one of my favorite refrigerator staples! So grab a carton and check out some of my favorite egg recipes below!
