Summary

  • US college students get 1 year of Google AI Pro free (verify via SheerID; auto-renews at paid rate afterward)
  • Students outside US get 1 year Google AI Plus (Gemini Omni, 400GB); exclusions apply.
  • New student hub + Gemini creates custom study notebooks, quizzes, 3D visuals and Deep Research; redeem by 31 Dec 2026.

It’s nearly time to head back to school, but there’s no need to be worried this year - Google is making it easier than ever to study for your exams. To help lighten the load during the September semester rush, Google is offering students across the USG an entire year of Google AI Pro, normally priced at $19.99 per month, completely free of charge.

Google’s Handing Out Free Drive Storage, But You Might Miss Out

Sadly, it’s not for everyone.

​

How to claim your free year of Google AI Pro

You'll need to verify your student email via an app

To claim your free year of Google AI Pro, you will need to verify your active enrollment as a college student through SheerID. With this, you’ll get four times higher usage limits in Gemini, built-in AI assistance directly inside everyday tools like Gmail and Google Docs, a massive 5 TB of cloud storage, access to Gemini Spark, Google Health Premium, and more.

​

Don't worry if you're outside the United States! Although you won't be able to obtain a Pro subscription, Google is still giving you one year of Google AI Plus free of charge. The offer gives you access to Gemini Omni, twice the usage limits in Gemini, 400 GB of storage and other benefits. This applies to all students except those in the U.S., Bolivia, Albania, Canada, Macau, Hong Kong, and Tunisia.

​

If you want to bundle your study tools with the perfect distraction from studying, you can also bag yourself ad-free music and video streaming with up to 70% off a bundle of Google AI Pro and a YouTube Premium subscription.

​

Keep in mind that the offer is meant for individual student use rather than school-administered accounts, and unless you cancel beforehand, the plan will automatically convert to standard monthly pricing once your free promotional year concludes.

Google even has new study tools to help you ace the semester

In addition to the savings available with subscriptions, Google has introduced a centralized student hub to help keep coursework organized. Within this hub, you are able to upload your class notes and syllabus in order to create custom study notebooks. Gemini will then automatically analyze these reference files to produce pop quizzes, create short learning lessons, and track your progress over time.

If you’re more of a visual learner, Google Gemini can now produce interactive visualizations such as custom 3D simulations and data tables to help you understand complex topics. Moreover, Google is incorporating Deep Research directly into Gemini Live, so you can start comprehensive, multi-stage research reports that run in the background while you review the results personally.

You’ve got until December 31, 2026, to redeem these offers, so don’t wait until after the Christmas break; head into the new semester prepared for success. You’ll need a valid form of payment when you go to sign up, but you can cancel any time.
