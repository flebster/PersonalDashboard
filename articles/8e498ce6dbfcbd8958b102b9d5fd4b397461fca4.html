Rogers sub-brand Fido has rolled out its back-to-school discounts. Through Fido, students can get discounts on a wide array of mobile devices — not so much plans, though.

Like last year, there weren’t any major discounts on mobile plans, just the standard $5 autopay discount.

However, there are lots of mobile deals, and here are some of the top ones:

  • Samsung Galaxy A17 – $0 down, $0/mo with the Fido Payment Program and the Plus wireless plan after bill credits
  • Google Pixel 10 – $0 down, $0/mo with the Fido Payment Program and the Plus wireless plan after bill credits
  • Apple iPhone Air – $0 down, $34/mo with the Fido Payment Program and the Plus wireless plan after bill credits
  • Google Pixel 10 Pro – $0 down, $15/mo with the Fido Payment Program and the Plus wireless plan after bill credits
  • Samsung Galaxy S26 – $0 down, $52.08/mo with the Fido Payment Program and the Plus wireless plan after bill credits

You can check out the rest of the mobile deals here.

I should also note that all of these deals include 24 months of financing with 0 per cent APR, and taxes extra.

Some other “Student Approved” deals include a shared phone plan, which is two lines with 60GB for $95/mo (one plan is $50/60GB, the other is $45/60GB).
