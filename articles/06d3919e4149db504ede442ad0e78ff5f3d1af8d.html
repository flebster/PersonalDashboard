If you actually want the “smart” part of a smart dishwasher to pull its weight, this LG model is an easy standout. We like that WiFi and LG’s ThinQ app let you fire up or pause a cycle from the couch, check how much time is left, and get pinged when it’s done, while Alexa and Google Assistant support hands-free control. The app keeps an eye on water supply, drainage, and leaks, and sends alerts if something looks off. It also nudges you when it’s time for routine maintenance.

The smart features extend to how it runs, too. A sensor wash cycle automatically adjusts time and water based on how dirty your dishes actually are. LG’s one-hour wash and dry program also uses four multi-directional spray arms for deeper cleaning and then circulates heated air throughout the tub for faster drying.

An auto-vent feature pops the door open slightly at the end of a cycle to vent steam, and a status light projects onto the floor so you’re able to see when it’s done at a glance. These features combine to make cleaning your dishes a little smarter and less painful.

While the upper rack gets fold-down tines for odd-shaped glassware and bowls, the lower rack doesn’t, so it’s a bit trickier to load large pans and bakeware than on models with fully flexible lower racks. However, if you normally hand wash large pots and pans, this may not be an issue.
