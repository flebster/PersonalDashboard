If energy usage is your top priority, DELLA’s 12,000-BTU mini-split is one of the most efficient systems commonly available online. Its efficiency rating sits around 22–23 SEER2 (which is a newer efficiency standard that prioritizes real-world performance), and that means it uses significantly less electricity than many other entry-level mini-splits.

The inverter compressor automatically adjusts output instead of cycling on and off, which helps the unit maintain steady temperatures and reduces power consumption. (You’ll really notice this feature during periods of extreme weather that tend to spike utility bills.)

The unit is well-sized for bedrooms, offices, or small living areas, and it cools and heats spaces up to about 600 square feet. The built-in Wi-Fi control and quiet operation round out the package.

A few drawbacks: This unit almost certainly requires professional installation, and while DELLA has great reviews, it’s a smaller company and may offer less customer support, based on some user feedback.
