The Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra2 was released at the July Unpacked event on the 22nd, showcasing the popular wearable’s extended battery life, improved outdoor performance, and a lighter design, putting it on the wishlists of many Samsung and Android users. To celebrate the launch, Amazon is offering a $100 gift card with Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra2 pre-orders that will ship out beginning August 7, bringing the price down by 13% to $699.99 (originally $799.99).

The 800mAh battery is around 35% larger than its predecessor, lasting around 60 hours with the display always on and around three days on lighter settings. And while it still can’t replace a Garmin, it’s significantly more rugged than the Ultra, with features like dual-frequency GPS, trail-run tracking with elevation data, hydration guidance, a titanium build, 10ATM water resistance, IP69K protection, and EN13319 dive certification. NB-NTN satellite connectivity is also an exciting improvement, letting you send messages or make calls without a cell signal.

The 47mm face has a 5,000-nit AMOLED display (up from 3,000 nits) for superior brightness and is powered by Qualcomm’s Snapdragon Wear Elite chip, built for AI workloads. The design is also lighter and more comfortable; despite the bigger battery, it’s now 12% thinner with a lighter strap, but still equally rugged with its titanium case. Compared to the Galaxy Watch 9,  the Ultra 2 is more durable and has a pretty major battery advantage.  

What do you think so far?

If you spend a lot of time outdoors and want a premium LTE smartwatch that can handle all your adventures (and will actually use the $100 Amazon gift card), this pre-order Samsung Galaxy Watch Ultra2 deal is one you should snag before it sells out. If you wait until launch day, you’ll pay the same price, but you won’t get the bonus gift card. That said, if you mostly just want notifications, fitness tracking, and sleep data, the Galaxy Watch 9 might be a more sensible choice.
