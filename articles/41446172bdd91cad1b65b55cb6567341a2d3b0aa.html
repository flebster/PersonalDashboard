Google Photos has introduced two new settings for its iOS and Android apps, allowing users to turn off grid dates and disable the sticker shimmer effect. The updates come directly in response to feedback from users looking for a cleaner media timeline.

The first update addresses the timeline layout. Users can now toggle date headers on or off in the main photo grid, providing a seamless stream of images without date interruptions.

Shimrit Ben-Yair, VP & GM, Google Photos, Google One and AI Subscriptions, said today, “Feedback received and papercuts fixed! We’ve just rolled out two small fixes in Google Photos based on your requests.”

How to turn off photo grid dates

To hide date headers in the main gallery view:

  1. Open the Google Photos app.
  2. Tap your profile picture or initials in the top right corner.
  3. Select Photos settings > Preferences > Photos view.
  4. Toggle Show dates in grid on or off.

The second update tweaks the sticker creation tool launched last year, which lets users cut out subjects from photos. While the tool uses a shimmer effect to highlight isolated subjects, users can now turn that animation off entirely.

How to disable the sticker shimmer effect

To turn off the shimmer animation:

  1. Open the Google Photos app and ensure you are signed in.
  2. Tap your profile picture or initials at the top right.
  3. Tap Google Photos settings, then select Preferences.
  4. Choose Photos view and toggle off Show shimmer.

Both features are rolling out now across mobile platforms, giving users more direct control over visual preferences in their media library, said Google earlier this week.
