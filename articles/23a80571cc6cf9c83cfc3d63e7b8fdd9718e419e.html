Road trips can be a budget-friendly and exhilarating way to see the world, but things can go wrong, fast. Even the most reliable vehicles are susceptible to air leaks and flat tires, and that’s typically a pretty huge buzzkill. A portable tire inflator is a must, and Etenwolf makes a fantastic one. It’s portable, powerful, and quite literally a lifesaver.

The S1 features a high-performance motor with a maximum pressure of 160 PSI and a 20L/min airflow, making it twice as fast as most devices on the market; you can fill a standard car tire in about a minute. Another major highlight: a long-lasting 5200mAh high-capacity battery. To put that in perspective, it can inflate 17 car tires, 60 motorcycle tires, 70 bicycle tires, 80 balls, and more on a single charge. It’s pretty well designed, especially from a portability standpoint. The hose can be neatly stored on the back of the tire pump, and there are dedicated slots for the ancillary accessories. It stows away in seconds and is perfect for long road trips, camping excursions, and everything in between.
