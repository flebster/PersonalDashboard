Alexa, Google Home, and Siri all talk to this A/C, which is more than I can say for units twice its price. I didn’t expect a budget-friendly A/C to out-app pricier units on this list, but the AC515S does. If you're on a budget and want convenience and modern technology, this is one of the better bang-for-buck units I've used. Its power and features surprised me. It also packs in more voice-assistant options, on top of the Dreo app and physical remote.

That said, the physical remote is so handy that I end up using it more than the app most days. It has buttons to change speed and temperature, set air modes, run the sleep timer, and turn on the auto-swing louver, and I love that the unit has a magnet on top for convenient remote storage between uses.

The sleep curve function is a nice touch, too. It gradually adjusts the temperature overnight instead of holding one setting all night, and the app also tracks room temperature and humidity in real time, which comes in handy on muggy days.

Manual controls on the front of the unit and the easy-to-see digital display show room temperature, mode, and fan power, so I always know what’s going on with it whether I’m using the remote, the app, or adjusting it by hand. Plus, drainage-free cooling means I don’t have to think about emptying a tank, and it has a dedicated noise-isolation system around the compressor that keeps it at a quiet 46 decibels.

The only catch is coverage. At 300 square feet, it's built for a bedroom or office, not a living room or larger space, so match it to the right room before you buy.
