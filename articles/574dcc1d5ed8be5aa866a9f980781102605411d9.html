Wondering what to watch on streaming?

Every week, MobileSyrup outlines some of the most notable new movies and TV shows. We typically focus on new content from Amazon’s Prime Video, Crave, Disney+ and Netflix, but other services like Apple TV+ and Paramount+ will be mentioned when relevant. Premium video-on-demand (PVOD) platforms are also fair game as movies continue to come to digital early.

Finally, we’ll highlight shows or movies that are made by Canadian companies, involve notable Canadian cast or crew, and/or are filmed in Canada.

Apple TV

Snoopy Presents: There’s No Place Like Home, Snoopy [Apple Original]

Apple TV premiere date: Friday, July 31
Genre: Animated special
Runtime: 31 minutes

In the latest Peanuts special co-produced by Vancouver’s WildBrain (The Snoopy Show), Snoopy must find his dog house with Charlie Brown after it’s sold in a yard sale.

Stream Snoopy Presents: There’s No Place Like Home, Snoopy on Apple TV.

An Apple TV membership costs $14.99/month and is also included in Apple One subscription bundles, which start at $22.95/month.

Crave

Montreal, My Beautiful 🇨🇦

Original theatrical release date: February 13, 2026
Crave premiere date: Friday, July 31
Genre: Drama
Runtime: 1 hour, 57 minutes

A Chinese immigrant in Montreal falls for a Québécoise woman.

Montreal, My Beautiful was directed by Montreal’s Xiaodan He (A Touch of Spring) and stars Joan Chen (Didi) and Montreal’s Charlotte Aubin (Isla Blanca). The film was also shot in Montreal.

Stream Montreal, My Beautiful on Crave.

An ad-free Crave Premium membership costs $22/month. Alternatively, there are two other subscription options: Crave Basic with Ads ($9.99/month) and Crave Standard with Ads ($14.99/month). Starz is available as a separate $5.99 add-on. You can learn more about subscriptions and add-ons on Crave’s website.

MobileSyrup also has round-ups of everything that’s coming to Crave in July and August.

Disney+

Furious [Disney+ Original]

Disney+ Canada premiere date: Monday, July 27 (first three episodes, new episodes on Mondays)
Genre: Crime drama
Runtime: Eight episodes

An FBI agent pursues a mysterious female serial killer, only for their lives to intertwine as the lines between right and wrong begin to blur.

Inspired by the 1987 film Black Widow, Furious was created by Elizabeth Meriwether (The Dropout) and stars Emmy Rossum (Shameless), Lola Petticrew (Bloodlands), Scoot McNairy (Narcos Mexico) and Quincy Tyler Bernstine (Ray Donovan).

Stream Furious on Disney+.

Disney+ costs $8.99/month for Standard with Ads, $15.99/month or $159.99/year for Standard (no ads) and $16.99/month or $169.99/year for Premium.

MobileSyrup also has round-ups of everything coming to the service in July and August.

Netflix

The Bombing of Pan Am 103 [Netflix Original]

Netflix Canada premiere date: Thursday, July 30
Genre: Drama
Runtime: Six episodes

A dramatization of the British and American governments’ investigations into the tragic bombing of a transatlantic flight over Scotland in 1988.

The series was created by novelist Jonathan Lee (Joy) and stars Connor Swindells (Sex Education), Eddie Marsan (Happy-Go-Lucky), Peter Mullan (I Swear), Toronto’s Patrick J. Adams (Suits) and Tony Curran (Outlaw King).

Stream The Bombing of Pan Am 103 on Netflix.

WWE Summerslam 2026 [Netflix Live Event]

Netflix Canada premiere date: Saturday, August 1 and Sunday, August 2 (6 p.m. ET/3 p.m. PT each day)
Genre: Wrestling
Runtime: Two days (a few hours each day)

WWE’s big annual Summerslam returns for a two-night event this year. Featured wrestlers include CM Punk, Cody Rhodes, Roman Reigns, Seth Rollins, Nikki and Brie Bella and Paige.

Stream WWE Summerslam on Netflix.

Netflix Standard with Ads costs $7.99/month, Standard (no ads, HD-supported) costs $18.99/month, and Premium (no ads, 4K-supported) costs $23.99/month. More information is available on Netflix’s website.

MobileSyrup also has round-ups of everything coming to Netflix Canada in July and August.

Paramount+

The Drama

Original theatrical release date: April 3, 2026
Paramount+ Canada premiere date: Friday, July 31
Genre: Romantic black comedy
Runtime: 1 hour, 45 minutes

The week before their wedding, a couple’s relationship is tested when the man finds out a secret from the woman’s past.

The Drama was written and directed by Kristoffer Borgli (Dream Scenario) and stars Zendaya (Euphoria) and Robert Pattinson (The Odyssey).

Stream The Drama on Paramount+.

Paramount+ Basic (with ads) costs $6.99/month or $61.99/year, Standard costs $10.99/month or $97.99/year, and Premium costs $13.99/month or $124.99/year. MobileSyrup also has round-ups of what’s coming to Paramount+ in July and August.

Prime Video

Batman: Caped Crusader (Season 2) [Amazon Original]

Prime Video Canada premiere date: Friday, July 31 (all episodes)
Genre: Animated superhero
Runtime: 10 episodes

In the second season of this 40s-set series, Batman must contend with new threats, including the Joker.

Batman: Caped Crusader was created by Bruce Timm (Batman: The Animated Series) and features the voices of Hamish Linklater (Midnight Mass), Jason Watkins (The Crown), Eric Morgan Stuart (Reality Bytes) and Matthew Needham (House of the Dragon).

Stream Batman: Caped Crusader on Prime Video.

The Super Mario Galaxy Movie

Original theatrical release date: April 1, 2026
Prime Video Canada premiere date: Friday, July 31
Genre: Animated comedy
Runtime: 1 hour, 38 minutes

Mario and friends must embark on a galaxy-hopping adventure to fight Bowser and his son.

Based on Nintendo’s Mario games, The Super Mario Galaxy Movie reunites The Super Mario Bros. Movie directors
Aaron Horvath and Michael Jelenic with stars Chris Pratt (Mario), Charlie Day (Luigi), Anya Taylor-Joy (Peach) and Jack Black (Bowser) with new voices Benny Safdie (Oppenheimer) as Bowser Jr., Brie Larson (Room) as Rosalina and Glen Powell (Anyone But You) as Fox.

Stream The Super Mario Galaxy Movie on Prime Video.

Prime Video is included with an Amazon Prime membership, which costs $99/year. An additional $2.99/month fee is required to remove ads.

MobileSyrup also has round-ups of everything coming to the service in July and August.
