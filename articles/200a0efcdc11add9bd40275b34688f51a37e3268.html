The Google Pixel Watch 4 is the latest model, at least until the Google event this August, when a 5th generation could debut. With that event around the corner, it's not surprising to see the current latest model discounted. Right now, the 41mm LTE Pixel Watch 4 version is $349.99 (originally $449), down to the lowest price the device has yet reached, according to price-tracking tools.

All Pixel Watch 4 models deliver faster charging times compared to the Pixel Watch 3, plus satellite SOS, dual-band GPS (for the LTE models), and a new circular display.

In her review, Lifehacker Senior Health Editor Beth Skwarecki praised the Google Pixel Watch 4 for its larger display, fast charging capabilities, and a redesigned charging dock. Speaking of charging, it only takes around 30 minutes to fully charge the device; 15 minutes will take it from 0% to 50%. That's one of the fastest charge times you'll find on any smartwatch. The new design also has an expanded circular display that reaches up to 3,000 nits of brightness.

With this LTE version, you'll get some nice features that the wifi version doesn't offer, including the ability to send satellite SOS messages to emergency services and your emergency contacts, even if you don't have cellular service. Most of the other features are pretty similar to the Pixel Watch 3: You get built-in Gemini for voice control, accurate sleep tracking, and exercise data, a heart rate monitor, a blood oxygen monitor, a temperature sensor, a pedometer, a gyroscope, and a barometer.
